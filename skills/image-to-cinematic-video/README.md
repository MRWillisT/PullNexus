# image-to-cinematic-video

A creative direction skill for turning still images into fully-planned cinematic video concepts — covering scene structure, camera motion, pacing, and storytelling beats.

## What it teaches

- Analyse a still image for mood, subject, and compositional potential
- Translate image elements into camera movement language (dolly, pan, rack focus, etc.)
- Build a shot-by-shot breakdown with timing and transitions
- Suggest music/sound design that reinforces the visual tone
- Output production-ready shot lists compatible with video AI tools (Runway, Kling, Sora)

## When to use

- Turning a photo or illustration into a video concept
- Pre-visualization for short films or social media content
- Briefing a video editor or AI video tool with a structured prompt
- Storyboarding for creative pitches

## Example invocations

```
Turn this landscape photo into a 30-second cinematic opener.
I have a portrait shot — what kind of video could I make from this?
Give me a shot list for an emotional product reveal using this image.
```

## Format

ShareGPT JSONL — 6 conversation pairs covering different image types and video styles.
